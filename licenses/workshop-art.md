# Planetary imagery in the personal notes scene

`assets/img/workshop/earth-color.jpg` is NASA Earth Observatory's Blue Marble:
Next Generation January base map, at 5400 × 2700. Imagery by Reto Stöckli,
NASA Earth Observatory.

Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/

Original: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/january/world.200401.3x5400x2700.jpg

`lunar-color.jpg` and `lunar-height.jpg` come from NASA's Scientific
Visualization Studio CGI Moon Kit, created from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter
camera and laser altimeter data. Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.
Visualizer: Ernie Wright (USRA); scientist: Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC).

Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

Originals:

- https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg
- https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/ldem_3_8bit.jpg

NASA media guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

Other sky, planetary portraits, and terrain maps are original procedural
artwork generated by `scripts/bake-workshop-art.py` and
`scripts/bake-workshop-surfaces.py`. No third-party rendering or image requests
occur at runtime; all assets are served by the site.
