EVAN SHI / A FEW NOTES

Opening the workshop…

Hello, I'm Evan

A little about me.

I'm Evan (Bowen) Shi. I work in games at 2K, and I studied computer science at RPI and Yale.

These are a few notes about games I've helped make, time spent teaching, and my interest in graphics. The more formal version is in my résumé.

Irvine · since July 2025

These days, games.

I joined 2K as a software engineer through its Graduate Engineering Program. Before that, I studied both computer science and Game Simulation Art and Science at RPI.

Games have been a thread through school projects and, now, my everyday work.

Language & stories

Can a model tell a joke?

At RPI, my research included humor generation and visual stories shaped by emotion and character traits. Later, at Yale, I worked on multi-document instruction-following.

During my AgentLive internship, that work became more practical: retrieval, image-generation APIs, and trying different language models.

A small part of that summer

A small testing tool.

At AgentLive, I also made a web interface for testing. Alongside the model experiments and APIs, there was the ordinary work of checking whether things behaved as expected.

This was a tool for that part of the process.

RPI · Submitty

The software behind a class.

I worked on Submitty, the open-source system used for assignments, exams, and grades. My contributions included PDF annotation, code differences, and auto-grading.

Faculty feedback helped shape the changes. It was software tied to the daily routines of a classroom.

RPI & Yale · office hours

On the other side of the desk.

I spent several years mentoring and teaching alongside my studies: labs and office hours at RPI, then data structures at Yale.

That meant answering questions, working with learning assistants, and writing assignment tests. A lot of my time at university happened outside my own classes.

Two games from school

A caterpillar, then pirates.

Finding Flora started with two course constraints: “forest” and “evolve.” Our team made a caterpillar that eats, climbs, and becomes a butterfly.

Next came Troubled Seas, a co-op pirate game. We didn't finish everything we planned, but the idea was friends figuring things out together aboard a ship.

An interest I keep returning to

Light, color, and geometry.

Some of my graphics projects have been about atmospheres, procedural worlds, and reconstructing objects from photographs. The space around these notes comes from that same interest.

I write about those experiments on my graphics blog.
