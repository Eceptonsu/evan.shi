How to play
HOW TO PLAY

Listen to the phrase, then repeat its notes in order. Use the piano or A S D F G H J K. A wrong note lets you retry; three misses end the run. Replaying costs the clean-round bonus, but helps you learn.

WHAT COUNTS

Correct notes earn 100 points once each. Finish a phrase on your first try, without replaying, for 150 extra. New phrases introduce more notes as you progress.
